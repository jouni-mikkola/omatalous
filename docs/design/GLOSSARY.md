# Glossary: one term for one thing, in Finnish and English

The terms the app shows for its pages, figures and actions, with the English of each. A label that is exactly a term
below has the English of this table in the English catalogue; `packages/core/test/glossary.test.ts` reads this table and
checks every message. The words not to use, the tone, dates and numbers are in `docs/UI_STYLE.md` (its Glossary
section holds the Finnish rules and the removed words). A new term is a row here and there in the same change.

Each row: the thing, the Finnish term, the English term. Only the first term in italics in a cell is checked.

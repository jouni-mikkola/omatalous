section: Money and reports
- Nyt shows more than the one sum. Under it: the month so far with the side of the usual the spending is on, the five
  largest categories as bars against last month to the same day, and what the household has and owes.
- A category bar on Nyt opens that category's rows of the month.
- Nyt names a loan that has no balance saved and links to where it is saved.
- A category with a purchase on an account last month lacks gets no comparison on Nyt.
- The balance and the bills the sum is made of stand right under the sum.

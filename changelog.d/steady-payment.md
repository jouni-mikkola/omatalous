section: Money and reports
- The monthly payment of a loan or credit is its regular instalment, in Laskut, the front page, the loan question and the reports. An extra repayment of capital is optional and is no monthly cost, and the figure stays the instalment as the history grows past a year.
- A payee's Swedish word beside the Finnish one ("Lainat Lån") is left out of the name in Laskut and Tapahtumat too.

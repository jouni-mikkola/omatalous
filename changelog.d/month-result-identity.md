section: Money and reports

- Yhteenveto names the savings and credit terms between Menot and Tulos, so they add up.
- Tulojen käyttö lists money taken from savings or drawn on credit; its shares add up to 100 %.
- Tapahtumat shows Menot, Tulot, Erotus and Siirrot ja säästöt, which add up to Tulos.

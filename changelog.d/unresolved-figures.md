section: Money and reports
- Cards whose paying account is not connected show in Tarkistettavat with one notice, and the first page's tile names the debt it subtracts. The notice returns only when another such card appears.
- Käytettävissä keeps taking a card's debt off whole when its payer is unclear, even when the debt is larger than the money, and Miksi? names the card and why.
- Miksi? and the data quality list say which balance no row can check and why, including the reasons that were silent before.

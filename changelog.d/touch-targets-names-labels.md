section: Look
- Links, buttons and checkboxes are larger to hit on a phone, and the small digits of a figure are readable.
- The tab title names the open report page and the open section of Asetukset.
- The month chart prints short month names, the year under January and hatches a month that is not whole.
- Tarkistettavat shows in the header only while something is open.
- The author and donation links moved from the page foot to Asetukset > Tiedot.
- Asetukset > Palvelin ja ylläpito no longer says "Ei automaattisia hakuja" beside a fetch schedule, and an action not yet run shows no note.
- A difference under a euro keeps its cents instead of showing as zero.
- Browsers on plain http no longer get the cross-origin isolation headers.

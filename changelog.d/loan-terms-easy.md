section: Money and reports
- A loan on a euribor with its review day needs only its margin: the form shows the rate now and the loan is saved at it.
  Without a review day the typed rate is kept.
- A loan found in the bank rows starts its form with the payment, interval, repayment type and review day the rows show.
  A review cycle is read only from two or more changes of the payment.
- A loan saved in Tarkistettavat shows its rate and balance before the next question.
- The loan form has one field named Korko, repayment types that say what changes, and no lines of help.

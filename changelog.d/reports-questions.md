- Reports opens with three questions: where the money goes, how money and debts develop, and how shared spending is split. On a phone they stand open as a short list.
- Income and Fixed costs are reports under the question about where the money goes, listed under its name.
- The balance line of Accounts and balances stops where the balance is unknown. It draws no line and no fill across unknown days, and marks a single known day between them with a dot.

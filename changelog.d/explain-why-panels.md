section: Money and reports
- A figure that opens Miksi? now shows it: a dotted underline and a question mark.
- The line under a Miksi? title says what the figure rests on, such as a usual month of the last 12 months.
- A bill that comes every few months says in Miksi? why its month is a part of the bill.
- A card payment older than one statement gap no longer counts as an unshown payment.
- Miksi? answers repeat faster while the data stays the same.

section: Money and reports
- Tarkistettavat no longer asks about a single small payment to an unknown payee.
- Koron pääomitus, Korko and similar interest credits count as Osingot ja korot on a deposit account, not on a card or a loan.
- A charge made twice is asked about only when it went to a business.
- The opening balance of a joint account counts as Yhteistä and is not asked. An answer given to it before stays in force.

section: Money and reports
- Tapahtumat opens on the running month. The period chip still shows the whole history.
- Kauppiaat is the one view by merchant. The grouping by merchant in the list is gone.
- A merchant paid in fewer than three of the last twelve months has no figure per month.
- Card bill payments are not merchants and no longer rank among them.
- On a phone the filter button sits under the last row and covers no amount.
- On a phone each row names its account by its digits, and an own transfer has a quiet amount.

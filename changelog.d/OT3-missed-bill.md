section: Money and reports
- Laskut keeps a bill that did not come in its list with "Ei tullut" and the day it was due, for about a month and a half or
  until the payments say it ended. Before, a monthly bill was late for five days and then moved among the ended.
- Tarkistettavat asks about such a bill for the same days. It takes "Lopetettu" for the series, or "Tulee vielä" for
  that one charge, which leaves the rest of what the app says of the series as it was.

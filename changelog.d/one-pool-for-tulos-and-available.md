section: Money and reports
- Käytettävissä now includes your part of the joint account, the same accounts Tulos counts.
- A loan paid from a savings account is counted once, as saving. The Menot tile, the month's curve, the usual month's line on the bars, the pace against the usual month and the spending goal on the first page all leave it out, so they add up with Tulos, and the payment no longer lowers Tulos a second time. Spending by category, spending by month and the loan figures still show the payment, and name it as Säästöistä maksettu beside the first page's Menot.
- A card paid only from accounts that Käytettävissä does not count no longer lowers it by its debt.

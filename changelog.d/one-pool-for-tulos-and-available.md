section: Money and reports
- Käytettävissä now includes your part of the joint account, the same accounts Tulos counts.
- A loan paid from a savings account is counted once, as saving. The Menot tile, the month's curve and Tulos on the first page add up, and the payment no longer lowers Tulos a second time. Spending by category, spending by month and the loan figures still show the payment, and name it as Säästöistä maksettu beside the first page's Menot.
- A card paid only from accounts that Käytettävissä does not count no longer lowers it by its debt.

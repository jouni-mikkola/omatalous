- Yhteenveto follows its design: the month's three figures are in the large size and line up when a label wraps, and on a phone each is a row with its label at the left. Tulevat kuukaudet keeps its four columns (Kuukausi, Tulot, Laskut, Jää) beside the month with a link to Laskut, and Varat ja velat stands under it. A thin rule sets the answer off from its working.

section: Money and reports
- Jää palkkapäivänä and Alin saldo on Yhteenveto expect the everyday spending of the days to the payday at the base months' even daily pace, instead of what fell on the same dates. Jää palkkapäivänä keeps every row of a debt in that spending. The lowest balance and the daily figure leave out the debt part of the payments of a loan the bill calendar walks by its plan, since the walk takes the plan's row. The lowest balance is looked for from today to the payday only, and the line under the figure shows when it is lower than Jää palkkapäivänä or unknown.

section: Money and reports
- Lainat shows no 12-month interest total while any loan that owes has no rate.
- Lainat, Varat ja velat and the holdings block show no debt total while a loan's balance is dated after today.
- Lainat shows no debt path while a debt that owes cannot be projected.
- A card whose statement is paid in full costs no interest in Lainat and is paid at its next statement, so it no longer hides the interest total and the debt path.
- The next payment of a loan with no plan is unknown when a missing rate could change it.

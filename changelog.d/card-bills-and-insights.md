section: Money and reports
- Card bills without a breakdown have the same name in every report of spending, and the spending chart shows them as their own bar with a link to the statement import.
- A new insight lists the bills that come once or twice a year by month and names the monthly reserve for them. Its line is twelve months of the household's gate, so a larger household needs larger bills.
- A new insight names the date the loans are paid off at the current pace and the interest left to pay. It is told only when every loan has what its plan needs.
- A new figure, "Säästöaste omista tuloista", tells the savings rate on income without payments from people, and an insight sets it beside the savings rate.
- A new insight tells which category is clearly above its usual level on the same days of the earlier months.
- A new insight tells the bills due on payday itself against the usual pay.
- The insights on the savings rate, the category pace and the bills on payday carry no yearly sum, since they are no money the household gains or loses by acting, and come after the ones that have one.

section: Money and reports
- A card purchase no longer pairs with money in from a person, and a payment to a person pairs with money in from a person only when an own account or an account holder's name says they are one movement.
- Money to or from your own name with no matching row is counted as saved, not as income or other spending, and the data quality card sees it.
- Money back from a wallet written with a Finnish case ending, such as "Revolutista" or "Revolutin", is a transfer.
- Money in from one payer becomes money from a person when at least two of their rows are already recognised that way and most of the recognised rows agree.
- A payment to an account of your household that arrives a little short, up to 2 % and 5 euro under, is a transfer for what arrived, not income on the receiving account. Where both accounts are in the view the difference is the payer's bank fee, under Pankkikulut. Among several payments that could pair, the closest sum pairs.
- In a member's own view where every account counts whole, money a holder pays into a joint account is a transfer, not income, unless the row names a payer account that is none of the household's. The household view pairs the two legs instead.
